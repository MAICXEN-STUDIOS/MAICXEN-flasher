How Hybrid USB-FOTA Works Read

Native USB-FOTA — Probes the connected MAICXEN EDGE device without triggering a bootloader reset and streams the firmware into the secondary OTA partition with per-chunk CRC-16/CCITT integrity verification.

Bootloader Fallback — For blank ESP32/ESP32-S3 modules or legacy builds, automatically switches to esptool-js while preserving stored Wi-Fi, Matter, and NVS partitions.
